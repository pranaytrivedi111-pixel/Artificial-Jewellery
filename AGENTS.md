## Strict Brand Integrity & Exclusive Identity Rules

1. **Zero Tolerance for Third-Party / Marketplace / Dropshipper Brand Names**:
   - **NEVER** mention "Meesho", "GlowRoad", "Shopsy", "IndiaMART", "TradeIndia", "Udaan", "ShopClues", "Snapdeal", "AliExpress", "DHgate", "Temu", "Shein", or any other wholesale/dropshipping/reseller/third-party brand name anywhere in the entire codebase, UI copy, metadata, titles, descriptions, badges, reviews, customer media, alt tags, FAQs, or customer communications.

2. **Automated Sanitization**:
   - All dynamic strings, product titles, descriptions, review comments, search queries, and share messages must continue to be routed through `brandSanitizer.ts` to ensure any accidental third-party marketplace or reseller wording is automatically replaced with luxury QAVELLE equivalents.

3. **Luxury Presentation & UI Standards**:
   - Maintain the premium, direct-to-consumer luxury jewelry aesthetic of QAVELLE.
   - All product packaging, hallmark certifications, anti-tarnish guarantees, and master artisan statements must reflect QAVELLE's direct atelier standards.
